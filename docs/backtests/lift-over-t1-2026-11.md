## Reading it (hand-written)

**Verdict: `unproven`. Three of the four built bots are already over t 1 on 2010+ (13D 1.61, buyback 1.37, US-sector gate 1.32), the country gate cannot be lifted, and the blend gets to t 2.1, which is not evidence.**

- **Cash yield is worth +0.1 to +0.3 in t, not more.** It matters for the mostly-cash bots: buyback_sp100 (62% cash on average) goes
  from t 1.37 to 1.65 and +4.3% to +5.2%/yr; the 13D bot barely moves (1.61 to 1.65). This is not new edge: the live copier already parks idle
  weight in SHV, so it is the part of the copied book that `portfolio_worth` of a paper bot does not show.
- **The country-ETF gate cannot be lifted: from 2010 on it earns nothing.** Alpha -0.2%/yr, t -0.10 (H1 -3.8%, H2 +2.4%), and +0.1% with
  T-bill cash. Its backtest numbers (t 2.32 over 1996-2026, 1.67 in the framework run from 2003) are the early, bear-market-heavy years. That is the
  clearest evidence yet that this bot is unlikely to look like its backtest live.
- **Global-sector gate:** t 0.50, 0.61 with cash. It stays under 1 on this window and I did not build it.
- **The blend lifts t because the event bots are independent of the gate bots, not because any bot got better.** The QQQ-residuals of
  buyback_sp100 and activist_13d correlate 0.02-0.07 with each other and with the gate books; the two gate books correlate 0.53 (US sectors and
  countries). An equal-weight blend of the four built bots gives +6.1%/yr, t 1.97, beta 0.36 with cash at 0%, and +6.5%, **t 2.12**,
  H1 t 1.25 / H2 t 1.74, with cash in T-bills. That would meet the project bar on paper (t >= 2, both halves positive, beta < 0.5).
- **Why it still is not evidence.** (1) Selection: these four were built *because* they looked best in studies of this same 2010-2026 data, so
  their blend is biased upward by exactly the amount of the selection; the discarded ideas (macro calendar legs, insider clusters, news, mid/small
  buybacks) are not in it. (2) Nearly all of the alpha comes from two streams whose own tests failed their pre-registered bars: the 13D book
  (+17%/yr, t 1.6, outlier-driven) and the S&P 100 buyback book (placebo p ~0.02 but event-level median negative). (3) The blend has no
  live days. (4) The 13D stream trades small and mid caps, where the assumed 10 bp per side may be optimistic.
- **What this means in practice.** The lift is available without writing a bot: the copier's `LIVETRADE_BOT_WEIGHTS` already blends bots and
  parks the rest in SHV. The only honest test is the live record, and the blend's alpha will be measured by `alphareport` only after the
  components accumulate months of live days. Not changed: no copier weights were touched.
