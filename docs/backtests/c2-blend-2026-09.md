# C2 blend — backtest, 2026-09-22

The Collective2 strategy (155809898) now copies three low-beta sleeves, each
weighted by the inverse of its volatility:

| Bot | Weight |
|---|---|
| TARegimeMultiAssetBot | 0.39 |
| GoldenButterflyMomBot | 0.37 |
| TSMOMTrendBot | 0.24 |

It replaces Kronos 0.34 / RegimeAdaptive 0.33 / EarningsInsiderTilt 0.33, which
had live alpha +3% at t 0.25. Kronos's forecasts show no measurable signal.

## Method

`scripts/onetime_c2_blend_backtest.py`:

1. **Sleeves:** each bot runs through `backtest_bot` on yfinance daily data from
   2017. Warmup bars are excluded, and the default costs apply (5 bps slippage
   plus the no-trade band). FearGreedInverse is simulated from CNN's Fear & Greed
   history with the live 30/50 rule.
2. **Weights:** inverse volatility, **fit on the first half only (2019-01 → 2023-06)**.
   The second half never informs the weights.
3. **Scoring:** alpha vs QQQ in each half and over the full sample.

Idle cash earns 0% here. Live, the copier parks it in SHV, so the live book
should do slightly better than this by roughly rf × the cash share.

## Verdict

- **This is the first candidate in the fleet with full-sample t ≥ 2** and positive
  alpha in both halves. That is why it is live.
- **FearGreedInverse is left out.** Its alpha is ~0 and it is the most QQQ-like
  sleeve (beta 0.34). Adding it lowers the blend's t-stat.
- **The diversification gain is smaller than hoped.** The three sleeves correlate
  at 0.58–0.67, because all are multi-asset, trend-flavoured and hold overlapping
  ETFs. The blend's t of 2.02 is barely above TARegimeMultiAsset alone (1.94).
  What the blend buys is less dependence on any one bot's parameters.
- **It is a low-risk, low-return sleeve.** At beta 0.13 it will lag QQQ badly in
  any rally, and that is expected (see CLAUDE.md). Its value is uncorrelated
  alpha. Scaling it up (for example 2x via margin) is only defensible once the
  live record confirms the backtest. Re-score it on `portfolio_worth` after
  ~6 months.
- **The first weeks are not steady state.** TARegimeMultiAssetBot started from
  cash on 2026-09-21 and funds a sleeve only on a buy signal. Until signals
  fire, its 39% sits in SHV.
