## Reading it (hand-written)

**Verdict: `unproven`, and no new bot.** The mechanism transfers weakly; it does not clear the bar anywhere outside the US.

- **Replication is close but not exact.** The US-sector row gives gated-by-SPY alpha +4.7%, t 2.61, beta 0.18; the bot's own
  backtest (quantsmd-v3-2026-11.md) reports about +5.5%, t 3.08. This simulator has no no-trade band and starts in late 1999
  because QQQ does, so its t is lower. Treat the other rows as slightly conservative relative to the bot's backtest, not as a
  different method.
- **On the US sectors the gate itself adds little to alpha** (gated minus ungated: +0.5%, t 0.32). What it does is cut the
  worst drawdown from -36% to -20%. A plausible reason (not checked) is that the SMA50/200 sector filter already moves to cash in most bear
  markets, so the 200-day SPY gate is largely redundant for alpha. That qualifies the v3 write-up, which credited the gate for the bear-market avoidance.
- **Outside the US the gate does add**, with the same sign in both halves: global sectors +2.9%/yr (t 1.5-1.6), countries
  +3.3%/yr own-index gate (t 1.9; H1 +3.0%, H2 +5.3%) and +1.7% with the SPY gate (t 1.0, H1 flat). Neither is individually significant.
- **No non-US book clears the project bar.** Global sectors: t 0.94-0.99. Countries, gated by their own index: t 2.32, beta 0.20,
  but H2 alpha is -0.6% and the whole alpha is the 1996-2006 stretch (+12.6%, t 2.9). From 2013 on it is -4.2% (2013-19) and
  +3.7% (2020-26), neither significant.
- **Same pattern as the US bot:** the alpha is episodic and comes from the early, bear-market-heavy years. In 2013-2019 the
  gated books earn at most +1.8% against QQQ (US sectors) and lose on the others (-1.1% global sectors, -4.2% to -6.1% countries). So this is more evidence for "a
  drawdown filter that pays in bear markets", not a new source of alpha, and it does not turn the SmaDailyGated candidate into
  more than `unproven`.
- **Not done: gate overlay on the live bots** (RegimeAdaptive, EarningsInsiderTilt). Their live record is 134 days and their
  decisions are not reproducible on 20 years of history (LLM allocation, insider data that was broken before 2026-09-22), so
  there is nothing honest to overlay the gate on.
- **Caveats:** the ETF universes are survivors (all still trade), the country ETFs include emerging markets with a large
  currency component, and costs here are a flat 5 bp or 20 bp per unit of turnover, with cash at 0%.
